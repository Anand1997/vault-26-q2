# Chess Training: Week 1 Sprint

> [!tip] Cognitive Reset Protocol
> 
> **Trigger:** Attention slipping or feeling the urge to make an impulsive move.
> 
> **Action:** Step away from the board for 15 seconds. Look at the physical edges of your monitor. Manually recount the pawns and pieces on the board to reboot your working memory and ground yourself.

## Day 1 [[2026-05-25]]
- [ ] Practice **King and Queen vs King** checkmate against the computer for 15 minutes.
- [ ] Review the first three moves of the 
	- Italian Game : `1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4`.
- [ ] Memorize the cognitive reset protocol (see above).
## Day 2 [[2026-05-26]]
- [ ] Practice **Two Rooks vs King** checkmate against the computer for 15 minutes.
- [ ] Play a training game, focusing strictly on controlling the center with active pieces.
- [ ] Execute the cognitive reset protocol immediately if you catch yourself making an impulsive move.
## Day 3 [[2026-05-27]]
- [ ] Practice **King and Queen vs King** checkmate against the computer for 15 minutes.
- [ ] Play a training game, making **king safety** (castling and pawn shields) the top priority.
- [ ] Execute the cognitive reset protocol if your attention drifts from the board.
## Day 4 [[2026-05-28]]
- [ ] Practice **Two Rooks vs King** checkmate against the computer for 15 minutes.
- [ ] Play a training game, focusing entirely on **piece activity** and ensuring pieces work together.
- [ ] Execute the cognitive reset protocol if overwhelmed by the middlegame complexity.
## Day 5 [[2026-05-29]]
- [ ] Practice **King and Queen vs King** checkmate against the computer for 15 minutes.
- [ ] Play a training game, attempting to deploy the **Italian Game** opening setup.
- [ ] Execute the cognitive reset protocol if you begin to move without calculating.
## Day 6 [[2026-05-30]]
- [ ] Practice **Two Rooks vs King** checkmate against the computer for 15 minutes.
- [ ] Play a training game using **"Backwards-to-Forwards"** thinking: stop to make mental notes of long-term goals before moving.
- [ ] Execute the cognitive reset protocol if your working memory feels overloaded.
## Day 7 [[2026-05-31]]
- [ ] Alternate between **King/Queen** and **Two Rooks** checkmates against the computer for 15 minutes to solidify muscle memory.
- [ ] Play a training game to integrate core system logic: center control, piece activity, and king safety.
- [ ] Review sprint progress and note any recurring focus issues in this daily note.
